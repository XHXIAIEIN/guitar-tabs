# Classical guitar samples

These samples contain recordings of a Spanish classical guitar by Roberto for FreePats.

- Source: [Spanish classical guitar](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html).
- Revision: `6f4eb1b092acc88f5448cea1a0001bd07b971af8` of [freepats/spanish-classical-guitar](https://github.com/freepats/spanish-classical-guitar).
- License: CC0 1.0. See [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).
- Conversion: mono, 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Filenames identify the original MIDI pitch.

The recordings retain their natural decay. Playback transposes the nearest sample to the score's pitch.
